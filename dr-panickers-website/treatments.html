General & preventive

Check-ups, cleaning and urgent care.

Specialist: All our dentists

Fillings & check-upsTooth-coloured repairs and routine care.

Tooth-coloured fillings repair decay and small fractures. A regular check-up and clean is what keeps most problems small.

Often suited to

Cavities, chipped or worn teeth, sensitivity, or anyone who has not had a check-up in a while.

What usually happens

  1. Examination, with X-rays where they are needed
  2. Decay removed under local anaesthetic
  3. Tooth-coloured composite placed and shaped
  4. Bite checked and polished

Cleaning & scalingRemoving what brushing cannot.

Professional removal of plaque and hardened tartar above and just below the gum line, finished with a polish and advice on the areas you are missing.

Often suited to

Everyone, at regular intervals, and sooner if your gums bleed or you notice staining.

What usually happens

  1. Examination of teeth and gums
  2. Ultrasonic scaling
  3. Stain removal and polishing
  4. Brushing technique reviewed with you

Emergency dental careSevere pain, swelling or a broken tooth.

For severe toothache, facial swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or a crown that has come off. Please call before you travel so we can advise you.

Often suited to

Anyone in pain now, or with an injury to the teeth or mouth.

What usually happens

  1. Call the clinic and describe what has happened
  2. Advice over the phone
  3. Examination and pain relief
  4. Definitive treatment planned once you are comfortable

Endodontics

Root canal care that saves natural teeth.

Specialist: Dr. Parvathy P, Endodontist

Root canal treatmentWhen decay or injury reaches the nerve of a tooth.

The infected pulp inside the tooth is removed, the canals are cleaned and sealed, and the tooth is rebuilt so it can be used for chewing again. Our endodontist also handles retreatment of earlier root canals, with work carried out under magnification.

Often suited to

Lingering pain with hot or cold, a darkened tooth, a deep cavity or a dental abscess.

What usually happens

  1. Local anaesthetic before anything starts
  2. Infected pulp removed and the canals cleaned and shaped
  3. Canals sealed and the tooth filled
  4. A crown where the tooth needs extra strength

Implants & restorative

Replacing and rebuilding teeth.

Specialist: Dr. Kishan Panicker G, Implantologist · Dr. Shreya B, Prosthodontist

Dental implantsA fixed replacement for one or more missing teeth.

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone. Once it has fused with the bone, it carries a crown that replaces the lost tooth without involving the teeth beside it.

Often suited to

One or more missing teeth, a failing bridge, or loose dentures you would like to replace with something fixed.

What usually happens

  1. Examination, X-rays and planning
  2. Implant placed under local anaesthetic
  3. A healing period while the bone fuses to the implant
  4. Final crown made and fitted
  5. Reviews and maintenance

Related: Full mouth rehabilitation Dentures Crowns & bridges

Ask about dental implants on WhatsApp
Crowns & bridgesProtecting weak teeth and closing single gaps.

A crown is a custom cap that covers a cracked or heavily filled tooth. A bridge uses the teeth on either side of a gap to carry a replacement tooth.

Often suited to

Cracked, heavily filled or root-treated teeth, and single gaps where an implant is not the right choice.

What usually happens

  1. Examination and a digital scan or impression
  2. Tooth prepared and a temporary crown fitted
  3. Final crown made and shade-matched
  4. Fit and bite checked before cementing

Related: Root canal treatment Dental implants

Ask about crowns and bridges on WhatsApp
Full mouth rehabilitationRebuilding the whole bite as one plan.

When many teeth are missing, worn or failing, implants, crowns and bridges are planned together so that chewing, comfort and appearance are restored as a whole.

Often suited to

Extensive tooth loss, severe wear, long-term denture wearers, or several failing teeth at once.

What usually happens

  1. Full assessment, X-rays and photographs
  2. A staged written plan
  3. Surgical phase where implants are needed
  4. Temporary teeth while healing takes place
  5. Final teeth fitted and reviewed

Related: Dental implants Dentures

DenturesRemovable replacements, made to fit.

Removable replacements for some or all of the teeth, adjusted until they sit comfortably. They can also be designed to clip onto implants for a firmer hold.

Often suited to

Several missing teeth, or an older denture that has become loose or uncomfortable.

What usually happens

  1. Impressions or a digital scan
  2. Try-in to check bite, fit and appearance
  3. Final denture fitted
  4. Adjustments as the gums settle

Braces & aligners

Straightening teeth and correcting the bite.

Specialist: Dr. Roopa R Tubaki, Orthodontist

Clear alignersRemovable, transparent trays.

A series of clear, removable trays, each moving the teeth a little further. They come out for eating and brushing.

Often suited to

Mild to moderate crowding, spacing or rotation, usually in adults who prefer not to wear visible braces.

What usually happens

  1. Digital scan and treatment planning
  2. Trays worn most of the day and changed on schedule
  3. Regular reviews to check progress
  4. Retainers to hold the result

Related: Braces Smile design

Ask about clear aligners on WhatsApp
BracesFixed, closely supervised correction.

Fixed brackets and wires apply steady, controlled pressure. Often the right choice for complex bite problems and for growing children and teenagers.

Often suited to

Significant crowding or bite problems, and children or teenagers who need guided correction.

What usually happens

  1. Assessment, X-rays and photographs
  2. Brackets bonded and the first wire fitted
  3. Adjustments every few weeks
  4. Braces removed and retainers fitted

Cosmetic dentistry

Shape, shade and proportion.

Specialist: Dr. Anuroopa P, Cosmetic & aesthetic specialist

Smile designPlanning the whole smile, not one tooth at a time.

Proportion, shade and gum line are planned together and discussed with you before treatment begins, combining whitening, bonding, veneers or gum work as needed.

Often suited to

Worn edges, gaps, uneven teeth, a gummy smile or old, discoloured dental work.

What usually happens

  1. Photographs, a scan and a conversation about what you want
  2. A proposed plan to review together
  3. Treatment carried out in agreed stages
  4. Final review

Related: Veneers & bonding Teeth whitening Clear aligners

Ask about smile design on WhatsApp
Veneers & bondingCorrecting shape, chips and colour.

Thin ceramic veneers or sculpted composite bonded to the front of the teeth, removing as little natural tooth as possible.

Often suited to

Chipped or worn front teeth, small gaps, misshapen teeth, or staining that whitening cannot shift.

What usually happens

  1. Assessment and design
  2. Minimal preparation where needed
  3. Veneers made, or composite sculpted in the chair
  4. Bonding, bite check and polish

Related: Smile design Teeth whitening

Ask about veneers and bonding on WhatsApp
Teeth whiteningProfessional, supervised whitening.

Lightens teeth that have darkened with age, tea, coffee or tobacco. In-clinic whitening uses the BlancOne light system, with the shade recorded before and after.

Often suited to

Healthy teeth and gums with general yellowing. Whitening does not change the colour of crowns or fillings.

What usually happens

  1. Teeth and gums checked first
  2. A clean, so the whitening works evenly
  3. In-clinic whitening session
  4. Shade recorded and aftercare explained

Gum care & oral surgery

The foundations that hold your teeth.

Specialist: Dr. Anuroopa P, Periodontist · Dr. Kishan Panicker G, Oral surgeon

Gum treatmentBleeding, receding or sore gums.

Bleeding gums are the earliest sign of gum disease. Treatment ranges from deep cleaning to laser therapy, carried out by a periodontist.

Often suited to

Bleeding when brushing, bad breath, receding gums, loose teeth or sensitivity along the gum line.

What usually happens

  1. Gum measurements to find where the problem is
  2. Deep cleaning under local anaesthetic
  3. Laser treatment where it is needed
  4. Review and a maintenance schedule

Related: Cleaning & scaling Dental implants

Wisdom tooth removalImpacted or repeatedly infected wisdom teeth.

Wisdom teeth that are impacted, partly erupted or repeatedly infected are assessed with X-rays and, where removal is advised, taken out by an oral and maxillofacial surgeon.

Often suited to

Pain or swelling at the back of the jaw, repeated infection, food trapping, or damage to the tooth in front.

What usually happens

  1. X-ray assessment
  2. Local anaesthetic
  3. Surgical removal
  4. Written aftercare and a review

Paediatric dentistry

Gentle care from the first tooth to the teenage years.

Specialist: Dr. Jaya Kiran, Paediatric dentist

Children’s dentistryGentle care from the first tooth.

Early visits are kept calm and simple so that children grow up comfortable with the dentist. Children are seen by a paediatric dentist.

Often suited to

Children of any age, from the first tooth through to the teenage years.

What usually happens

  1. A relaxed first visit
  2. Examination and a count of the teeth
  3. Fluoride or sealants where they help
  4. Brushing and diet advice for parents
