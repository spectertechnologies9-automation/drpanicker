The clinic

A calm place forcareful dentistry.

A neighbourhood clinic in AGS Layout, Uttarahalli, equipped for everything from routine check-ups to implant surgery, with specialists for each area of care.

01Inside the clinic

A look around.

The clinic as you will find it, from the street outside to the treatment room.

02Equipment

Chosen for precision and comfort.

What we use in the clinic, and why it matters to you.

  1. 01

    Intraoral scanner

    A digital scan of your teeth in place of impression trays and putty.

  2. 02

    RVG digital X-ray

    A digital sensor that shows the X-ray image on screen within seconds.

  3. 03

    Portable X-ray

    X-rays taken at the dental chair, without moving to another room.

  4. 04

    Work under magnification

    Fine work such as root canals and restorations carried out under magnification.

  5. 05

    Dental laser

    Used in gum and soft-tissue treatment.

  6. 06

    Cautery

    Controls bleeding and shapes gum tissue during minor procedures.

  7. 07

    Physiodispenser

    A precision implant motor with controlled speed and constant cooling.

  8. 08

    BlancOne whitening light

    In-clinic, light-activated teeth whitening.

03Patient care

How we look after you.

The right specialist

Six dentists, each in their own speciality, so gum problems are seen by a periodontist, root canals by an endodontist and children by a paediatric dentist.

Explained before it begins

Your dentist talks you through what they have found, the options and the costs, so you can decide before any treatment starts.

At a pace that suits you

If you feel nervous about dental visits, let us know when you book and your dentist will take you through each step.

04Your visit

From first message to aftercare.

  1. Get in touch

    Call or WhatsApp the clinic and we will find a time that suits you.

  2. Consultation

    Your dentist listens to your concerns and examines your teeth and gums.

  3. Your plan

    Findings, X-rays and options are explained, along with the cost.

  4. Treatment & aftercare

    Treatment carried out with aftercare advice and reviews where needed.
