Appointments

How do I make an appointment?

Call +91 99867 19039 or +91 77952 49359, or send a WhatsApp message to +91 99867 19039. The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:30 PM.

Can I ask to see a particular doctor?

Yes. Mention the doctor when you call or message, and the clinic will arrange a time with them. You can read about each dentist on the Our Doctors page.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. For severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or a crown that has come off, please call the clinic before you travel so we can advise you on what to do and when we can see you.

Treatments

How do I know which treatment I need?

Your dentist will recommend treatment after examining you. The Treatments page explains what each option involves, so you can come prepared with questions.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Children are seen by our paediatric dentist, Dr. Jaya Kiran. First visits are kept calm and simple, with preventive advice for parents.

Who places dental implants at the clinic?

Implants are handled by our implant specialists. Dr. Kishan Panicker G is an oral and maxillofacial surgeon and implantologist, and Dr. Shreya B is a maxillofacial prosthodontist and implantologist.

Clinic hours & location

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:30 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays.

Where is the clinic?

4/1, 3rd Main, AGS Layout, Uttarahalli, Bengaluru 560061. Open directions in Google Maps.

Costs & payments

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on what you need. After your examination, your dentist will explain the options and their costs so you can decide before any treatment begins.

Which payment options do you accept?

Please ask the clinic about payment options, including any instalment arrangements, when you call or message to book.

General information

What should I bring to my appointment?

Any previous X-rays or dental records, and a list of the medicines you take. If you feel nervous about dental visits, let us know when you get in touch.

I feel anxious about dental treatment. What can I do?

Let us know when you book and again when you arrive, so your dentist can talk you through each step before it happens.
