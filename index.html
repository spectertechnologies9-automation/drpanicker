All frequently asked questions
How do I make an appointment?

Call +91 99867 19039 or +91 77952 49359, or send a WhatsApp message to +91 99867 19039. The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:30 PM.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. For severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or a crown that has come off, please call the clinic before you travel so we can advise you on what to do and when we can see you.

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on what you need. After your examination, your dentist will explain the options and their costs so you can decide before any treatment begins.
