About Dr. Kishan Panicker G

Dr. Kishan Panicker G is an oral and maxillofacial surgeon and implantologist, and co-founder of Dr Panicker’s Dental Care in Uttarahalli, Bengaluru, alongside Dr. Anuroopa P. A Fellow and Diplomate of ICOI (Buffalo University, USA), he specialises in full mouth rehabilitation and implant treatment of the complex edentulous maxillary arch, with expertise in zygomatic and pterygoid implants and TMJ disorders. His approach is holistic, ethical and patient-centred.
